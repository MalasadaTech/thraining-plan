## 1. Key Concepts

**3.6.3** wrote a named-technique hunt. **3.2.2** taught the classroom card. This hour is the **site path** that makes a hunt official — who starts it, who owns it, where leads go.

This course **does not** publish a DYA hunt ticket, queue, or lead board. Every shop builds its own. A new hunter **obtains** the path.

| Path | You will be shown (locally) | You do **not** do this hour |
|------|-----------------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Initiate** | How a hunt is opened | Invent “file Hunt-17” |
| **Control** | Who can widen scope / pause / stop | Run a hunt because it is interesting |
| **Leads** | Where new leads are parked and who triages them | Stand up a personal spreadsheet as policy |

**What good looks like:** “I obtain the initiate / control / lead path from [role the instructor names, or my lead]. If none was shown: **I do not have the local process yet.** I do **not** open a hunt on a made-up ticket.”

“Not yet” is a pass.
