## Module Overview for Instructors

**Purpose of this module:**  
Obtain the local initiate / control / lead path. “Not yet” is a pass. Do not invent a ticket.

**Context (plain language):**

- What this hour is for: Hunters get the shop’s hunt-control path from the shop, not from classroom fiction.
- How it hooks to the hour before: 3.6.3 wrote a named-technique hunt.
- How it hooks to the hour after: 3.7.2 is where the hunt is written down.
- Why we are doing it this way: Short 0.x / 4.x voice. Outline a–b. Obtain-and-follow. Same rule as 2.12.
- What we are *not* doing this hour: Invent DYA tickets or a lead board. Rewrite 3.2.2. No lab.
- Extra step: none. If you overlay a real shop path, say it is overlay, not DYA policy.

**Key Teaching Points:**
- “I do not have the local process yet” is a passing answer.
- Interesting is not permission to start.
