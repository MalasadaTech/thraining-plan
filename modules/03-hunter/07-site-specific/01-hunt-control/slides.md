---

### Slide 2 – Why this lesson exists
**Title:** Why this lesson exists

A named technique is not permission to search live.

The shop decides how a hunt is **opened**, who **controls** it, and where leftover **leads** go.

That path is local. This lesson is how you obtain it and follow it.

**Speaker Notes:**  
This slide is the student intro. The job is obtain-and-follow. Do not start a hunt because it looks interesting. Do not invent a DYA ticket or board.

---

### Slide 3 – Initiate and control
**Title:** How hunts are initiated and controlled

**Initiate** — how a hunt is opened here. Who may start it, and on what path.

**Control** — who may widen scope, pause, or stop.

Every site builds its own. This course does not publish DYA’s.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Walk initiate and control first. Interesting is not permission. If you overlay a real shop path, say it is overlay, not DYA policy.

---

### Slide 4 – Lead management
**Title:** Lead management

A hunt **lead** here is leftover work that is not this hunt’s scope.

You obtain where leftovers are parked and who triages them.

You do not stand up a board. Hand-off of a finished hunt is **3.7.3**.

**Speaker Notes:**  
This is not extracting TTPs from a CTI report, and it is not the 3.7.3 hand-off chart. Parking leftovers is local. Do not invent a spreadsheet as policy.

---

### Slide 5 – Obtain, then follow
**Title:** Obtain, then follow

**Obtain:** ask the lead where the path lives. If none was shown: **I do not have the local process yet.**

**Follow:** use only the path you were shown.

Do **not** invent a ticket so the hunt looks official. “Not yet” is a pass.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Walk the two products from the student guide. Inventing a ticket number so the page is not blank is a fail. Do not rewrite the classroom card.

### Slide 7 – Summary
**Title:** Summary

Every shop has a path to initiate a hunt, control it, and manage leftover leads.  
Obtain that path. Follow what you were shown.  
If you do not have the process, write that. Do not invent a ticket or a board.
