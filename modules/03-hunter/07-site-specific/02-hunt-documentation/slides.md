# Module 3.7.2 – Hunt Documentation Standards  
## Slide Deck Content

**Target Audience:** Threat Hunter (primary); SOC Analyst, CTI Analyst (secondary)  
**Estimated Delivery Time:** 20–25 minutes  
**Total Suggested Slides:** 7

---

### Slide 1 – Title Slide
**Title:** Module 3.7.2 – Hunt Documentation Standards  
**Subtitle:** Obtain the form and the official place. Do not invent them.  
**Footer:** SOC / Hunter / CTI / DE Training Program

**Speaker Notes:**  
3.7.1 obtained how a hunt is opened and controlled. This lesson is how the hunt is written down. It is not a DYA template this course publishes, and it is not a rewrite of the 3.2.2 classroom card.

---

### Slide 2 – Why this lesson exists
**Title:** Why this lesson exists

Hunters write the hunt on the shop’s **form**, in the shop’s **official place**.

The next person has to find a complete record.

Obtain the local standard. Then write there.

**Speaker Notes:**  
This slide is the student intro. The job is obtain-and-follow. Do not start listing required fields. Do not invent a DYA template or an archive path.

---

### Slide 3 – Required elements
**Title:** Required elements

**Required elements** are what the site form always wants on a hunt write-up.

Every shop lists its own. A new hunter obtains them early.

This course does **not** publish DYA’s template.

The **3.2.2** classroom card is training. It is not the site form.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Required elements vary by site. The classroom card (hypothesis, scope, priority, unique pattern) is still useful as ideas. Treating it as shop policy is a fail. If you overlay a real shop form, say it is overlay, not DYA policy.

---

### Slide 4 – Where and how hunts are documented
**Title:** Where and how hunts are documented

The official place and method (sometimes called the **store**).

Your lead names it. This course does **not** publish that path.

Personal notes and Slack are **not** the hunt record.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Where and how is a site fact, not a folder path this lesson invents. Scratch notes can help you think. They are not the official record. Do not promote Slack as the store.

---

### Slide 5 – Document on the shown standard
**Title:** Document on the shown standard

**Obtain:** the required elements and where hunts are documented.

Write **there**, on that form.

If none was shown: **I do not have the local standard yet.**

Do not invent a template name or a folder path as policy.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Walk the two products. “Not yet” is a pass. Inventing a template so the page is not blank is a fail. Stay off initiate/control and off outputs and hand-off.

---

### Slide 6 – Knowledge Check
**Title:** Knowledge Check

1. The 3.2.2 classroom card is the shop’s official hunt form. True or false?  
2. What two things does this lesson obtain?  
3. What do you write if no one has shown you the standard?

**Speaker Notes:**  
Answers are only in the instructor guide. Three questions for the whole lesson. Do not add a fourth.

---

### Slide 7 – Summary
**Title:** Summary

Every shop has hunt documentation standards.  
Obtain the required elements and where hunts are documented.  
Write there. If you do not have the standard, write that.  
Do not invent a template.
