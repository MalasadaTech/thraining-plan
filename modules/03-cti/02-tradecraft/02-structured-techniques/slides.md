### Slide 6 – Classroom Pair
**Title:** Two Techniques

**KAC** — list must-bes; if false, then…  
**ACH** — 2+ hyps; mark evidence C / I / N

Others exist. Not sign-off here.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Outline b.

---

### Slide 7 – When
**Title:** Which First

Locked story, hidden must-bes → **KAC**  
Two live hyps + discriminating evidence → **ACH**  
No hyp, no judgment → **neither** (collect)

**Speaker Notes:**  
Outline c.

---

### Slide 8 – Read ACH
**Title:** Fewest I Wins

**I** = hard to square with that hyp.  
Do not score “how much I like H1.”

**Speaker Notes:**  
Usual fail.
