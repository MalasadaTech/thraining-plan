### Slide 8 – Two Axes
**Title:** Likelihood ≠ Confidence

**Likelihood** — how probable (this card)  
**Confidence** — how good the evidence (**3.1.7** low / medium / high)

*Likely*, **medium** confidence — both named.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Outline c.
