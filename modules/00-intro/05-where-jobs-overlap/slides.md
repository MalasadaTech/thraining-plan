### Slide 2 – What this hour is
**Title:** What this hour is

Same host, log, or domain. Different **product**. Two hats still two products.

**Speaker Notes:**  
You wanted smaller shops called out so people do not smash two jobs into one output. Today we only name that split.

---

### Slide 3 – Same evidence
**Title:** Same evidence

Everyone may look at the same **host**, **log**, or **domain**.

That does not make the jobs the same.

**Speaker Notes:**  
WS-JLEE can be the shared host. Do not write a ticket. Do not tell the PRD story.

---

### Slide 4 – Different product
**Title:** The product is different

Close or escalate an **alert**.  
An **intel note**.  
A **hunt**.  
A **rule**.

**Speaker Notes:**  
These are the four products from the outline. Do not teach how to write any of them.

---

### Slide 5 – Asking is not doing
**Title:** Asking is not doing

Asking the next desk is not that desk’s whole job.

One person may wear **two hats**. Still two products.

**Speaker Notes:**  
An RFI is a question, not the intel note. A hunt package is a hand-off, not the hunt. If they do two jobs, they still finish two products.

---

### Slide 6 – Knowledge Check
**Title:** Knowledge Check

1. Everyone may look at the same host. Does that mean they are doing the same job?  
2. Asking the next desk is doing that desk’s whole job. True or false?  
3. In a smaller shop, one person may write two products. Why does this outline still name the jobs separately?

**Speaker Notes:**  
Answers only in the instructor guide. Three questions for the whole lesson. Stop.

**Title:** Summary

Same evidence. Different product.

Two hats still two products.
