## Module Overview for Instructors

**Purpose of this module:**  
Name the order of the rest of the course, put DYA and PRD back on the board, and show what “name the next hand-off and whose product it is” looks like.

**Context (plain language):**

- What this hour is for: The map of the rest of the course, plus one look at the hand-off task. DYA and PRD get named again so they stay the course story, not a name from hour one.
- How it hooks to the hour before: 0.4 was same evidence, different product, and two hats.
- How it hooks to the hour after: the SOC analyst block starts (endpoint activity map, then process rows).
- Why we are doing it this way: You wanted PRD and DYA highlighted again. You also wanted detections before alerts, and the later story to be this same flow as one incident.
- What we are *not* doing this hour: Writing the companion story. Telling the PRD plot. Inventing tickets. No lab. Not teaching 1.1.
- Extra step: none.

Say **DYA** and **PRD** with the full names once, same as 0.1: Dixon, Yamada, & Associates; Pink River Dolphin. Fiction, not site policy.

**Key Teaching Points:**
- Order: SOC, then CTI, then hunting, then detection engineers.
- Detections before the alert queue. Site-specific later.
- DYA / PRD come back as one incident after the lessons.
- Next hand-off + whose product. Not the ticket name.

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

1. **Order of the four tracks?**  
   **Answer:** SOC analyst, then CTI, then hunting, then detection engineers.  
   **Explanation:** Outline a.

2. **DYA and PRD? When one incident?**  
   **Answer:** DYA is the course company (Dixon, Yamada, & Associates). PRD is the course adversary (Pink River Dolphin). Both are fiction. They come back after the lessons as one companion-story incident.  
   **Explanation:** Outline d. Same names as 0.1.

3. **Extra infrastructure — next hand-off and product?**  
   **Answer:** Whoever blocks (firewall / IA). Product is the block. Not a hunt.  
   **Explanation:** 0.5.1 with outline 0.3 e.
