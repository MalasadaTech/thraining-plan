---

### Slide 3 – Start of the path
**Title:** Alert, then triage

An analyst gets an alert and sorts what it is and what to do next.

Then: incident response, and notify leadership.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Triage means sort the alert — not write a novel. IR and leadership are next. An alert on WS-JLEE can start here. Do not tell the PRD story.

---

### Slide 4 – Ask intel
**Title:** RFI

**RFI** = Request for Information.  
Ask intel for more work on *that* alert.

Intel works it, adds context, and may find more infrastructure.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Do not teach how to write the RFI. Enrich means add context.

---

### Slide 5 – Then two kinds of hand-off
**Title:** Block vs hunt package

Extra infrastructure → whoever **blocks** (firewall / IA).

A **hunt package** → hunters, and to **detection engineers** (MDE, YARA, Suricata, SIGMA, and so on).

**Speaker Notes:**  
These are different outputs. Do not send the extra IPs to the hunt team as if that were the block. Two hats is 0.4.

---

### Slide 6 – Knowledge Check
**Title:** Knowledge Check

1. After triage, what two things can the analyst do with the alert besides asking intel?  
2. Extra infrastructure goes to the hunt team. True or false?  
3. Where can a hunt package go besides the hunt team?

**Speaker Notes:**  
Answers only in the instructor guide. Three questions for the whole lesson. Stop.

---

### Slide 7 – Summary
**Title:** Summary

Alert → triage → IR and leadership → RFI → enrich.  
Then: block extra infrastructure, and/or a hunt package to hunters and detections.

**Speaker Notes:**  
One possible path. Same path the companion story will retell later.
