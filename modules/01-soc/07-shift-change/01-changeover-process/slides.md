### Slide 5 – Purpose
**Title:** Why Structure It

Incoming crew takes the floor **without losing** open work, outages, or urgent policy.  
Hallway “you’re good” is not a changeover.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Outline a.

### Slide 6 – Who
**Title:** Classroom Roster

**Runs:** outgoing lead **Pat**  
**Receives:** incoming lead **Riley**  
**Attend:** owners of open cases (you = A12, Jordan = RFI)  
**IR:** **Sam** only if the incident is still in handoff

### Slide 7 – Where
**Title:** System of Record
