### Slide 5 – Buckets a–b
**Title:** Work That Moved

**a.** Still **open** / in progress  
**b.** **Opened, updated, or closed** *this shift*

Still-open ≠ what changed today.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Outline a–b.

---

### Slide 6 – Buckets c–d
**Title:** Two Outage Lines

**c.** **Planned** (not yet)  
**d.** **Ongoing** or **already happened** this shift  

EDR window tomorrow ≠ Zeek drop at 13:10.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Outline c–d.

---

### Slide 7 – Bucket e
**Title:** Urgent Process / Policy

New **do / do not** the next crew must know **now**.  
Its own line. Not a clause on A12.

Classroom: do not close Night Owl-related cases without IR concurrence.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Outline e.
