### Slide 5 – Incident Report
**Title:** Incident Report

Records a security incident (or strongly supported suspected incident).  
Case / IR handoff.

**Not** a question to another team.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Outline a.

---

### Slide 6 – RFI
**Title:** Request for Information

You need **data** from CTI, hunt, IT, or a vendor.  

Can sit **beside** an incident.  
It is not a second case record.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Outline b.
