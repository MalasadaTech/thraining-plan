**Title:** Escalate-for-More-Info

### Slide 7 – Status Words
**Title:** Met / At Risk / Breached

**Met** — inside the window (or sent)  
**At risk** — last third (optional)  
**Breached** — past the number  

Always name the clock.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Task.
