# Module 2.6.2 – Threat Relevance and Organizational Impact  
## Slide Deck Content

**Total Suggested Slides:** 8

### Slide 1 – Title
**Title:** Threat Relevance and Impact  
**Subtitle:** Why does this finding matter here?

### Slide 2 – Relevance
Ask whether the finding intersects:

- mission
- assets / identities
- technology
- exposure / access path
- observed local evidence

### Slide 3 – Impact
If the finding is true here:

**What plausible consequence changes for the organization?**

Keep the consequence proportional to the evidence.

### Slide 4 – Four separate questions
**Applicability:** can it happen here?  
**Visibility:** can we see it?  
**Relevance:** does it matter here?  
**Impact:** what changes if true?

### Slide 5 – A12 relevance
Windows workstation  
+ encoded PowerShell  
+ local observation

→ relevant to DYA

### Slide 6 – A12 impact
If update-domain activity was payload delivery:

→ contain / examine `WS-JLEE`  
→ determine transfer/execution

Do not claim what remains unresolved.

### Slide 7 – Knowledge Check
1. Applicability vs relevance?  
2. No visibility: automatically irrelevant?  
3. Write A12 relevance + impact.

### Slide 8 – Summary
**Does it matter here?**  
**What changes if true?**

Keep uncertainty visible.
