# Instructor Guide – Module 2.7.4 – RFI Responses and Closure

**Estimated Time:** 15–20 minutes

## Purpose

1. Write a direct RFI response that separates the supported answer from unresolved evidence gaps.
2. Check the response against the original requirement and record closure or agreed follow-up.

## Teaching Sequence

1. Retrieve the intake record created in 2.1.5. Briefly verify the question and needed-by time without repeating the intake lesson.
2. Ask learners to draft the answer before showing the worked response. Challenge any claim of successful delivery or execution unsupported by the case.
3. Check the answer against the requirement, evidence, uncertainty, and usefulness to the requester. A timely partial answer can meet the current need if its limits are clear and follow-up is agreed.
4. Record the closure or follow-up decision. Use 2.7.5 for delivery and 2.8.2 for actual site approval/archive procedures.

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

1. Accept a judgment of likely attempted payload delivery, supported by the request record, with an explicit statement that successful download/execution is not established.
2. The supported partial answer, evidence basis, uncertainty, and evidence needed to resolve the gap; include agreed follow-up where applicable.
3. Record a follow-on or revised requirement with the requester. Do not silently expand the original scope indefinitely.
