# Module 2.7.5 – Disseminating Intelligence  
## Slide Deck Content

**Total Suggested Slides:** 9

### Slide 1 – Title
**Disseminating Intelligence**  
Right audience, right channel, right sharing boundary

### Slide 2 – Three separate decisions
**Audience** – who needs it?  
**Channel** – how may it travel?  
**Sharing rules** – who may receive/redistribute?

### Slide 3 – TLP 2.0
TLP controls cybersecurity information-sharing boundaries.

It is **not** a classification system.

Reference: [FIRST TLP](https://www.first.org/tlp/)

### Slide 4 – TLP 2.0 meanings
**RED** → individual recipients  
**AMBER+STRICT** → recipient organization only  
**AMBER** → organization + clients, need-to-know  
**GREEN** → defined community  
**CLEAR** → no TLP disclosure restriction

Reference: [FIRST TLP 2.0](https://www.first.org/tlp/docs/tlp-a4.pdf)

### Slide 5 – Classroom A12 technical send
IR / SOC  
Approved incident system  
TLP:AMBER+STRICT

Include:
- host
- URI/path
- infra
- timing
- uncertainty

### Slide 6 – Classroom leadership send
Approved leadership/CTI channel  
Same core judgment:

**Likely attempted payload delivery**

Keep:
- status
- implication
- uncertainty

Remove unnecessary technical detail.

### Slide 7 – Tailor detail, not judgment
Technical and leadership versions may look different.

They should not disagree about:
- likelihood
- confidence
- known gaps

### Slide 8 – Additional instructions
TLP can be accompanied by sharing instructions where appropriate.

Reference: [FIRST TLP Use Cases](https://www.first.org/tlp/use-cases)
