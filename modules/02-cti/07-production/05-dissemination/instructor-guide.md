# Instructor Guide – Module 2.7.5 – Disseminating Intelligence to the Correct Audiences

**Estimated Time:** 20–25 minutes  
**Delivery Method:** Instructor-led explanation and discussion

## Purpose

Teach learners to route a finished product to the correct audience through an approved method with accurate sharing restrictions, while preserving the underlying analytic judgment.

## Critical TLP Correction

Under FIRST TLP 2.0:

- **TLP:AMBER+STRICT** = recipient organization only.
- **TLP:AMBER** = recipient organization and its clients, need-to-know.
- **TLP:GREEN** = defined community.
- **TLP:CLEAR** = no TLP disclosure restriction, subject to applicable rules/procedures.
- **TLP:RED** = individual recipients only.

Reference: [FIRST TLP 2.0](https://www.first.org/tlp/docs/tlp-a4.pdf)

Do not teach TLP:AMBER as “organization only.”

## TLP Is Not the Whole Handling Model

TLP does not replace:
- classification;
- privacy rules;
- legal/contractual restrictions;
- system authorization;
- local dissemination policy.

The channel question remains separate from the sharing-label question.

## Classroom A12 Route

**Technical**
- audience: IR/SOC
- approved method: classroom incident-management system
- sharing: TLP:AMBER+STRICT
- detail: host, URI/path, infrastructure, timing, caveat

**Leadership**
- audience: duty lead/leadership
- approved method: classroom approved CTI channel
- sharing: same restriction unless the originator changes it
- detail: concise judgment, status, uncertainty; omit unnecessary technical identifiers

## Common Student Challenges

| Challenge | Coaching response |
|---|---|
| Uses TLP:AMBER for org-only sharing. | Correct to TLP:AMBER+STRICT. |
| Treats TLP as classification. | Explain that TLP controls cybersecurity information sharing, not classification. |
| Sends through personal SMS because recipient is correct. | Audience and channel are separate decisions. |
| Removes “likely” for leadership. | Tailoring can remove detail, not uncertainty. |
| Shares every technical artifact with leadership. | Apply need-to-know/minimum-useful-detail thinking. |

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

1. **TLP:AMBER+STRICT**.
2. TLP describes sharing boundaries; approved channel describes how the organization authorizes transmission/storage.
3. Examples: hostname, hash, path, exact IP, detailed timestamps—assuming leadership does not need them for its decision.
