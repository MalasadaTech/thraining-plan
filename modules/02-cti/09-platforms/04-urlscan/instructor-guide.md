## Module Overview for Instructors

**Purpose of this module:**  
Read a URLScan card. Extract title / requests / IPs or write not on card. No live submit.

**Context (plain language):**

- What this hour is for: CTI analysts see what a URL served.
- How it hooks to the hour before: 2.9.3 was PDNS.
- How it hooks to the hour after: 2.10.1 is STIX objects. 2.9 ends.
- Why we are doing it this way: Short 0.x / 4.x voice. Outline a–b. Card only.
- What we are *not* doing this hour: 0.7 survey. Live submit lab.
- Extra step: none.

**Key Teaching Points:**
- Retrieve is enough.
- Screenshot is information.

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

1. **This hour is when to pick URLScan. True or false?**  
   **Answer:** False. 0.7.  
   **Explanation:** Stay-in.

2. **Two fields?**  
   **Answer:** Any two: title, final URL, requested hosts/IPs, redirect chain.  
   **Explanation:** Outline b.

3. **No card. What do you write?**  
   **Answer:** **Not on card** / no result. Do not invent a page.  
   **Explanation:** Task 1.
