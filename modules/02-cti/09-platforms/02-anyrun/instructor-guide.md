## Module Overview for Instructors

**Purpose of this module:**  
Search and review an AnyRun card. Extract who/what or not on card. No live account.

**Context (plain language):**

- What this hour is for: CTI analysts read a public detonation for a seed they have.
- How it hooks to the hour before: 2.9.1 was VT tabs.
- How it hooks to the hour after: 2.9.3 is Silent Push / PDNS.
- Why we are doing it this way: Short 0.x / 4.x voice. Outline a–b. Card only.
- What we are *not* doing this hour: 0.7 survey. Beacon POST plot. Live lab.
- Extra step: none.

**Key Teaching Points:**
- Search by the seed you have.
- “Malicious” tag is information.
