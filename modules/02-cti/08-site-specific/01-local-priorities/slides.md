# Module 2.8.1 – Local Intelligence Requirements and Priorities
## Slide Deck Content

**Total Suggested Slides:** 8

### Slide 1 – Title
**Local Intelligence Priorities**  
Find the source of truth before claiming alignment

### Slide 2 – This is an onboarding skill
Learn:
- where the current list lives
- who owns it
- how you know it is current
- how work maps to it

### Slide 3 – Current matters
Check:
- version
- effective date
- owner
- supersession
- status

Last quarter's copy may not govern today's work.

### Slide 4 – PIR is not the only label
Local shops may use:
- PIRs
- intelligence requirements
- standing requirements
- leadership priorities
- mission priorities

Use the local authoritative structure.

### Slide 5 – A12 is not automatically a PIR
Active incident ≠ formal priority requirement.

Alignment requires a stated local requirement.

### Slide 6 – If the list is missing
Write:

**Current priority alignment not yet verified.**

Then identify where the current list must be obtained.

### Slide 7 – Orientation note
Capture:
- source
- owner
- version/date
- review cadence
- mapping method

### Slide 8 – Knowledge Check
1. Why verify currency?  
2. No list yet: what do you record?  
3. What source metadata do you capture?
