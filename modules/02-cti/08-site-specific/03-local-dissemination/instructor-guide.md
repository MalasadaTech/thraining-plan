# Instructor Guide – Module 2.8.3 – Local Dissemination Channels and Customers

**Estimated Time:** 20–25 minutes  
**Delivery Method:** Instructor-led orientation and discussion

## Purpose

Teach learners to identify the organization's actual CTI customers and approved dissemination paths.

This module intentionally depends on local guidance rather than a generic external customer list.

## Learning Objectives

1. Locate the authoritative customer/channel map.
2. Route a product using that map or identify that the local path is not yet verified.

## Suggested Timing

| Part | Time |
|---|---:|
| Customer concept | 4 min |

## Detailed Teaching Notes

### Customer is an operating relationship

Help learners distinguish:
- an authorized recurring consumer;
- an occasional stakeholder;
- someone who simply might be interested.

The local CTI operating model should tell them who the function serves.

### Make the map concrete

If an authorized local overlay is available, have learners fill:
- product/use;
- customer;
- channel;
- handling/approval notes.

If no overlay is available, keep the blanks and teach what information must be obtained.

### Explain why channels exist

Approved channels often provide:
- access control;
- audit;
- retention;
- handling support;
- workflow linkage.

This is the reason behind the rule, not merely “because policy says so.”

### External partners

Require explicit local authorization.

The existence of a relationship with a partner does not imply every intelligence product can be sent to them.

## Common Student Challenges

| Challenge | Coaching response |
|---|---|
| Treats every stakeholder as a customer. | Ask what recurring/authorized CTI service relationship exists. |
| Chooses a convenient personal channel. | Ask what controls the approved channel provides. |
| Assumes an external partner should receive it. | Require an explicit local sharing rule/approval path. |
| Knows the customer but not the channel. | Both are required for a complete dissemination path. |

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

1. A CTI customer is an established/authorized intelligence consumer in the local operating model, not merely an interested person.
2. Product/use, primary customer, approved channel, handling/approval notes.
3. Record that the local dissemination path has not yet been verified and identify the owner/source needed to obtain it.

## Closing

This module closes the CTI block by connecting general tradecraft to the real organization the analyst works in.
