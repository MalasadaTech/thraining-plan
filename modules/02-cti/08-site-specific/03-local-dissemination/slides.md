### Slide 1 – Title
**Local Dissemination and Customers**

### Slide 2 – Local customer
A customer is an established intelligence consumer.

Not simply:
“someone who might like the report.”

### Slide 3 – Build the map
For each product:
- customer
- approved channel
- handling notes
- approval/escalation if needed

### Slide 4 – Why channels matter
Approved systems can provide:
- access control
- audit
- retention
- handling support
- workflow linkage

### Slide 5 – External sharing

- shareable products
- channel
- markings
- additional approval
