# Instructor Guide – Module 2.8.2 – Local Production and Approval Processes

**Estimated Time:** 20–25 minutes  
**Delivery Method:** Instructor-led orientation and discussion

## Purpose

Teach learners to map and follow the organization's actual production workflow rather than memorizing an invented classroom ticket or approval chain.

The local process is the authoritative reference for this module.

## Learning Objectives

1. Locate and explain the collection-request, review, approval, release, and archive workflow.
2. Follow the known process and identify missing onboarding information precisely.

## Detailed Teaching Notes

### Frame it as a workflow map

Instead of repeating “do not invent,” ask learners to build a map of the actual process:
- entry point;
- reviewers;
- approver;
- release;
- archive;
- version control.

### Separate collection need from local request mechanics

The analyst can identify an evidence requirement before knowing the shop's ticket system.

Use A12:
- need transfer evidence;
- local request method must come from the organization.

### Review vs approval

Ask:
- Who can improve/challenge the draft?
- Who has the authority to release it?

The answers may be the same role, but the concepts are different.

### Archive and versioning

The learner should identify the authoritative product repository and how superseded versions are handled.

This is important for reproducibility and later review.

## Common Student Challenges

| Challenge | Coaching response |
|---|---|
| Invents a plausible Jira queue. | Ask where that queue is documented locally. |
| Treats review as approval. | Separate quality review from release authority. |
| Thinks analytic completion means workflow completion. | Ask whether the product is approved and stored as the official version. |
| Doesn't know one process element and guesses it. | Record the specific onboarding gap and the role/source needed to close it. |

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

1. Planning defines what evidence is needed and how it might be collected; the local request process is the organization's mechanism for obtaining/coordinating it.
2. Review quality and release authority are distinct functions even if one local role performs both.
3. Record that the authoritative archive location is not yet verified and identify who/process documentation must provide it.
