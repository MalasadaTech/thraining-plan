## Module Overview for Instructors

**Purpose of this module:**  
Obtain the shop list. Align or write “not yet.” Do not invent PIR-01.

**Context (plain language):**

- What this hour is for: CTI analysts get the current local priorities from the shop, not from a classroom fiction list.
- How it hooks to the hour before: 2.11.3 answered the RFI.
- How it hooks to the hour after: 2.12.2 is the local produce/approve path.
- Why we are doing it this way: Short 0.x / 4.x voice. Outline a–b. Obtain-and-follow.
- What we are *not* doing this hour: Invent DYA PIRs. Rewrite 2.1.4. No lab.
- Extra step: none. If you overlay a real shop card, say it is overlay, not DYA policy.

**Key Teaching Points:**
- “I do not have the list yet” is a passing answer.
- A12 aligns only if the card says so.

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

   **Answer:** Write **do not have the list yet**. Ask the lead where it lives.  
   **Explanation:** Task 1.
