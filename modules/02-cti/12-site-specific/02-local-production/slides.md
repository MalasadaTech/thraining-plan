---

### Slide 2 – Why this lesson exists
**Title:** Why this lesson exists

A finished draft does not leave the desk just because you wrote it.

CTI analysts follow **this shop’s** process to request collection, get a product approved, and store the official copy.

Obtain that process. Follow it. Or write that you do not have it yet.

**Speaker Notes:**  
This slide is the student intro. Every shop names tickets, reviewers, and archive locations differently. A new analyst obtains that process early. Do not invent a DYA board so the class has a form.

---

### Slide 3 – Workflow, reviews, authorities
**Title:** Workflow, reviews, authorities

**Workflow** — the sequence from a collection request or a draft through review to an approved product.

**Reviews** — someone reads the draft against shop standards before it is official.

**Approval authorities** — the role allowed to approve so the product can leave as official.

You obtain those names. You do not invent them.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Name the three ideas. Stop. Do not draw a chain of reviewers or a ticket system. A form called INT-REQ-01 and a DYA Jira board are invented. The finished draft itself stays in 2.11.1.

---

### Slide 4 – Request, archive, or not yet
**Title:** Request, archive, or not yet

**Collection request** — file the local request. Planning collection is **2.1.8**.

**Archive** — the official copy is stored to local standards. Do not invent a folder path.

If no process was shown: **I do not have the path yet.**

That line is a pass.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Show this before the knowledge check. Filing a request is not the collection plan. Not yet is legal. If you overlay a real shop process, say it is overlay, not DYA policy.

---

### Slide 5 – Do not invent the process
**Title:** Do not invent the process

No `INT-REQ-01` as policy.  
No DYA Jira board.  
No change board as policy.  
No made-up archive folder.

Follow the names you were shown.  
Do not rewrite the product (**2.11.1**).

**Speaker Notes:**  
The stay-in rule for this unit is obtain-and-follow. Classroom invention is not org policy. Local customers and channels are 2.12.3.
