## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. Say that a **local produce / approve / archive** path exists, and how you **obtain** it.
2. Follow that path — or record that you **do not have it yet**. Do not invent a ticket name.

**Mapped Proficiency Items:**
- K: 2.12.2 – Local production and approval processes

## 1. Key Concepts

CTI analysts **follow the shop path** to request collection, get a product stamped, and archive it. The *product* is **2.11.1**. The *collection plan* is **2.1.8**. This hour is **where that path lives**. Do not invent a DYA Jira board or approval chain.

| You do | You do not |
|--------|------------|
| Ask who reviews, who archives, what the collection-request form is called **here** | Invent “INT-REQ-01” as policy |
| Follow the card if the instructor overlays one | Pretend a change board exists |
| Write **do not have the path yet** | Fill a classroom ticket as org policy |

**What good looks like:** “I obtain the produce/approve/archive card from my lead. If they show a form, I use that name. If not: **I do not have the path yet.** I still do not invent a board.”
