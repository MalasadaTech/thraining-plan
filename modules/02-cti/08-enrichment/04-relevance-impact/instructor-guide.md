## Module Overview for Instructors

**Purpose of this module:**  
Two sentences: applies here, and what would change. No PIR. No country.

**Context (plain language):**

- What this hour is for: CTI analysts write the so-what for *this* shop.
- How it hooks to the hour before: 2.8.3 handled the IOC.
- How it hooks to the hour after: 2.9.1 is VT Relations — a tool, not impact.
- Why we are doing it this way: Short 0.x / 4.x voice. Outline a–c. Law firm, not OT.
- What we are *not* doing this hour: PIR list. TTP extract. Attribution. No lab.
- Extra step: none.

**Key Teaching Points:**
- Relevance ≠ PIR.
- Impact is concrete (IR has the host), not “crisis.”
