# Module 2.5.7 – Correlation, Link Analysis, and Campaign Tracking

## Slide Deck Content

**Total Suggested Slides:** 6

### Slide 1 – From records to relationships

Combine findings without dropping source, time, or uncertainty.

### Slide 2 – A usable link record

object A | relationship | object B | evidence/source/time | alternative | assessment

### Slide 3 – Corroboration

Check distinctiveness, temporal relevance, and source independence.

### Slide 4 – A12

Rare NS + time-overlapping IP supports an infrastructure relationship; campaign and actor claims require more.

### Slide 5 – Maintain tracking

Record scope, confidence, new evidence, contradictions, and assessment changes.

### Slide 6 – Summary

Promote relationships only as far as the evidence supports. Keep candidate links, campaign assessments, and actor attribution distinct.
