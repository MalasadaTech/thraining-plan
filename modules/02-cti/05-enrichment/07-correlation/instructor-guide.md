# Instructor Guide – Module 2.5.7 – Correlation, Link Analysis, and Campaign Tracking

**Estimated Time:** 20–25 minutes

## Purpose

1. Combine enrichment records into evidence-backed links while testing alternative explanations.
2. Distinguish a candidate relationship, an activity-set or campaign assessment, and actor attribution.

## Teaching Sequence

1. Reuse the learners' enrichment records. Draw or list only the links supported by those records; attach a source and time to each edge.
2. Compare one shared provider field with multiple distinctive, time-relevant findings. Ask for the best alternative explanation and whether the sources are independent.
3. Have learners write a bounded link assessment, then identify the additional activity evidence needed for a campaign claim.
4. Keep file/behavioral relationships separate from the DTF infrastructure classifications. Evaluate the analysis rather than the appearance of a graph.
5. Revisit a stale or contradicted relationship. Require an assessment update that preserves the historical observation and explains the change.

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

1. No. Preserve both citations if useful, but identify the shared upstream source and avoid double-counting independence.
2. Record a candidate or assessed infrastructure relationship with sources, time, hosting context, and limitations. A campaign claim needs additional evidence of related activity and an objective/time scope, not just a graph of domains.
3. No. Preserve the label as source context; seek technical or temporal evidence and assess attribution separately.
