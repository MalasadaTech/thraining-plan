# CTI Analyst – Course Map

Begin after the SOC section summary (1.6). The sequence follows the work: define the question, evaluate and collect evidence, assess its significance, then produce and deliver an answer.

Platform guides use two passes: a short retrieval orientation in 2.4, followed by detailed interpretation and assessment alongside the relevant 2.5 method. Complete the whole platform lesson across those two passes; do not count its duration twice.

Keep one A12 evidence record through the sequence: RFI intake → collection question → source/provenance → enrichment and links → applicability/impact → supported answer and follow-up.

## Instructor delivery notes

- **RFI intake and response:** capture the question in 2.1.5; answer that same question in 2.7.4. Intake is not repeated as a second lecture in production.
- **Enrichment and analysis:** teach IOC handling first, then file/registration/DNS methods, generic infrastructure pivots, DTF application, and correlation. Assess organizational significance in 2.6.
- **Frameworks:** use ATT&CK, Diamond, and Kill Chain to organize analysis. Add DTF identifiers to an already justified infrastructure pivot; keep file and behavioral pivots separate.
- **Local application:** use actual local priorities and approved collection/handling rules when first needed, then complete the local workflow at the end.
- **Progression:** proficiency levels are retained for moved tasks. Split RFI and link-analysis lessons make existing requirements easier to teach and assess; they do not increase the required role levels.
