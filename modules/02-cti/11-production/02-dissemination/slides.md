### Slide 3 – Audience, channel, marking
**Title:** Audience, channel, marking

**Audience** — who owns the next decision. IR / SOC (technical) vs leadership (awareness).

**Channel** — ticket or approved intel path. Reject personal SMS, private chat, and public post.

**Marking** — a label that says who may see this.  
**Caveat** — an extra instruction the label does not say.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Name the three things before anyone picks a chat thread. A caveat is not a second color. Example: no hash on the leadership send. SOC incident routing is 1.5.3, not this slide.

### Slide 4 – Classroom card, not org policy
**Title:** Classroom card, not org policy

**TLP:AMBER** — need-to-know inside the organization. Not public.  
**TLP:CLEAR** — no restriction. Not for a product that names a live host.

A one-liner is still marked.

If the shop has a real card, use it.  
Do not invent `TLP-RED-DYA`.

**Speaker Notes:**  
These names are lesson-only. Overlay a shop card if they have one. TLP here is Traffic Light Protocol used as a practice label, not a DYA policy.
