## Module Overview for Instructors

**Purpose of this module:**  
Route the finished product: audience, approved channel, marking. Reject SMS. Overlay a shop card if they have one.

**Context (plain language):**

- What this hour is for: CTI analysts put the product on a path the shop already approved.
- How it hooks to the hour before: 2.11.1 wrote the product.
- How it hooks to the hour after: 2.11.3 is answering the RFI itself.
- Why we are doing it this way: Short 0.x / 4.x voice. Outline a–c. Classroom markings only.
- What we are *not* doing this hour: Invent a DYA TLP scheme. Local customer list. Rewrite the judgment. No lab.
- Extra step: none.

**Key Teaching Points:**
- Ticket / approved channel. SMS fails.
- Leadership: no hash.
