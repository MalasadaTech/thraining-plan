> What is known about the update domain, and does evidence support payload delivery?

The requirement drives collection.

### Slide 5 – Platform result ≠ intelligence
Platform result:
- sourced observation
- time
- object
- limitation

Intelligence:
- assessed answer
- confidence/uncertainty
- significance

### Slide 6 – Enrichment creates candidate links
Examples:
- file similarity
- shared NS
- overlapping IP
- certificate
- registration/DNS relationship

Candidate first. Stronger claims require stronger evidence.

### Slide 7 – Keep assessment terms separate
**Applicability** – can it happen here?  
**Visibility** – can we see it?  
**Relevance** – does it matter here?  
**Impact** – what could follow?

### Slide 8 – Keep claim strength proportional
**Candidate relationship**  
→ **assessed activity-set/campaign**  
→ **actor attribution**

Do not skip evidence levels.

### Slide 9 – Production closes the requirement
A strong answer includes:

- supported judgment
- evidence
- uncertainty
- unresolved gap
- closure/follow-up
- correct audience

### Slide 10 – Bridge to hunting
CTI asks:

> **What does the evidence mean?**

Hunting asks:

> **Does this behavior exist elsewhere internally?**
