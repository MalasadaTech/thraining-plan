## Module Overview for Instructors

**Purpose of this module:**  
Assess an attribution statement: type claimed versus evidence, and whether confidence is earned.

**Context (plain language):**

- What this hour is for: CTI analysts name a cluster they can defend, not a country they cannot prove.
- How it hooks to the hour before: 2.1.6 was how you say it. This hour is who you claim.
- How it hooks to the hour after: 2.1.8 is where you collect. Not the actor profile.
- Why we are doing it this way: Short 0.x / 4.x voice. Outline a–c. Classroom low / medium / high only. Story bible: “PRD APT” is a vendor label.
- What we are *not* doing this hour: Estimative word list. Actor profile. Invent a nation-state. No lab.
- Extra step: none.

Do not tell the PRD plot as proof of a government.

**Key Teaching Points:**
- Activity group ≠ nation-state.
- Label ≠ high confidence.

## Detailed Teaching Notes

### 1. Key Concepts

Write purpose / challenges / two types / three classroom confidence words. Walk the vendor-PDF claim as a fail.

If they write a country: “What evidence?”  
If they write the actor profile: “2.11.1.2.”  
If they argue likely/almost certainly: “2.2.1.”

---

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

1. **Vendor APT name = high nation-state. True or false?**  
   **Answer:** False. It is a label.  
   **Explanation:** Outline a / story bible.

2. **Activity group vs nation-state?**  
   **Answer:** Cluster of activity vs a government sponsor. You can defend a cluster without a country.  
   **Explanation:** Outline c.

3. **“Vendor PDF says PRD APT — high confidence nation-state.” Assess?**  
   **Answer:** Type over-claimed. Confidence too high. Evidence is a label. Activity group / low is the honest read until internals support more.  
   **Explanation:** Task 1.
