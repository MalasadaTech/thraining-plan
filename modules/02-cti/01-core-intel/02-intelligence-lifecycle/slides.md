### Slide 2 – Why this lesson exists
**Title:** Why this lesson exists

CTI analysts name **which job they are in** so a question becomes a used answer.

A paste into a TIP is not analysis. Missing material means collect again.

This lesson is the **loop** of stages, not the layer.

**Speaker Notes:**  
This slide is the student intro. Daily intel work is knowing whether you are still gathering, judging, or delivering. Do not write a priority intelligence requirement, pick source classes, or write a finished paper today.

---

### Slide 3 – Six stages
**Title:** Six stages

**Planning and Direction** — decide the question and what “done” looks like.  
**Collection** — gather the raw material against that question.  
**Processing and Exploitation** — turn raw intake into usable information.  
**Analysis and Production** — judge what it means and write the answer.  
**Dissemination** — get the answer to someone who can act.  
**Evaluation and Feedback** — learn whether they used it and what to do next.

Shops may rename stages. The work still has to happen.

**Speaker Notes:**  
One purpose line each. Exploitation here means make it usable, not exploit a host. Do not add a seventh stage. Collection as a stage is not OSINT vs commercial — that is 2.1.8.

---

### Slide 4 – A stage is a job, not a folder
**Title:** A stage is a job, not a folder

Putting a hash in a TIP is **processing**, not analysis.

A chat titled “INTEL” is not **dissemination**.

Collection gathers **data**. Processing turns it into **information**. Analysis produces **intelligence**.

**Speaker Notes:**  
The 2.1.1 layers sit inside three of the jobs. Planning, dissemination, and evaluation are jobs around that path, not extra layers. If they start listing source classes, that is the next cluster child, not this lesson.

---

### Slide 5 – The flow loops
**Title:** The flow loops

**Planning** — RFI: is this the payload host for campaign **A12**?  
**Collection** — pull the A record.  
**Processing** — store the IP in the TIP.  
**Analysis** — we assess it is; treat it as such.  
**Dissemination** — get that sentence to SOC.

No A record yet? **Back to Collection.** Not a guess to disseminate.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Walk the given before the knowledge check. Evaluation and Feedback is SOC using or ignoring the answer, which opens the next question. Do not write a requirement. Do not tell the intro plot.
