## Module Overview for Instructors

**Purpose of this module:**  
Name the stage and say the flow loops. A stage is a job, not a folder.

**Context (plain language):**

- What this hour is for: CTI analysts know which job they are in so they do not call a TIP paste “analysis.”
- How it hooks to the hour before: 2.1.1 was the layer. This hour is the loop around that path.
- How it hooks to the hour after: 2.1.3 is type (strategic / tactical). Not a stage.
- Why we are doing it this way: Short 0.x / 4.x voice. Outline a–b. Six course names. Shop may collapse names; the work stays.
- What we are *not* doing this hour: PIR format. Source classes. Finished product. No lab.
- Extra step: none.

Do not invent a seventh stage. Do not tell the PRD plot. **A12** RFI is enough.

**Key Teaching Points:**
- Six stages. Loop, not a slide deck.
- Collection is data. Processing is information. Analysis is intelligence.
- Missing material → back to collection.

**Required Materials:**
- Student Guide
- Slide Deck

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

1. **Dissemination is last and the work stops. True or false?**  
   **Answer:** False. Evaluation and feedback can open the next question. Analysis can send you back to collection.  
   **Explanation:** Task 2 (flow).

2. **Six stages?**  
   **Answer:** Planning and Direction; Collection; Processing and Exploitation; Analysis and Production; Dissemination; Evaluation and Feedback.  
   **Explanation:** Outline a.

3. **Pull the A record for the update domain against the A12 RFI. Stage?**  
   **Answer:** **Collection.**  
   **Explanation:** Outline b / task 1.
