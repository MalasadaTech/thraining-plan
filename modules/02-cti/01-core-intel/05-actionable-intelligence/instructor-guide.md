## Module Overview for Instructors

**Purpose of this module:**  
Say whether a product can be acted on, and why.

**Context (plain language):**

- What this hour is for: CTI analysts check the product, not the question again.
- How it hooks to the hour before: 2.1.4 wrote the requirement.
- How it hooks to the hour after: 2.1.6 is how you say it to a given audience.
- Why we are doing it this way: Short 0.x / 4.x voice. Outline a–b. No invented five-box shop rubric.
- What we are *not* doing this hour: Audience rewrite. Hunt-useful test. Actor profile. No lab.
- Extra step: none.

Stay on **A12**. Do not invent a DYA “actionable form.”

**Key Teaching Points:**
- Answers the requirement + who + what.
- “Be aware” fails.

- Student Guide

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

1. **“Be aware” is actionable. True or false?**  
   **Answer:** False. No next step.  
   **Explanation:** Outline b.

2. **Two fail reasons?**  
   **Answer:** Any two: does not answer the requirement; no who; no specific what; too late; no caveat.  
   **Explanation:** Outline b.

3. **A12 payload-host sentence. Actionable? Why?**  
   **Answer:** Yes. It answers the RFI and names IR / treat-as-such.  
   **Explanation:** Task 1.
