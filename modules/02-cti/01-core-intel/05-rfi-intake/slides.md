# Module 2.1.5 – RFI Intake and Prioritization

## Slide Deck Content

**Total Suggested Slides:** 7

### Slide 1 – RFI intake

Turn a request into a bounded question and a justified next action.

### Slide 2 – Receive

Requester · question · decision/use · needed-by · scope · handling · related case

### Slide 3 – Evaluate

Clear? CTI-owned? Already answered? What evidence exists, and what is missing?

### Slide 4 – Prioritize

Use local policy, decision urgency, mission importance, dependencies, and available evidence.

### Slide 5 – A12

A request for /update.exe is established. Successful transfer and execution remain unresolved.

### Slide 6 – Carry forward

Keep the intake record with the evidence. Answer the same question in 2.7.4.

### Slide 7 – Summary

A clear RFI establishes the question, its purpose, and its limits. Record evidence gaps and priority before collection begins.
