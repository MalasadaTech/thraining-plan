# Instructor Guide – Module 2.1.5 – RFI Intake and Prioritization

**Estimated Time:** 15–20 minutes

## Purpose

1. Capture a bounded RFI with its decision need, scope, deadline, and handling context.
2. Evaluate answerability, identify missing evidence, and justify priority or routing using local policy.

## Teaching Sequence

1. Connect the SOC reporting handoff to the requirement just developed in 2.1.4.
2. Work through receive, evaluate, and prioritize using the A12 request. Have learners complete one intake record. Supply fictional requester/deadline values explicitly as classroom assumptions or mark them for clarification.
3. Compare the active-incident RFI with routine background research. Ask for the reason for priority, not an invented SLA.
4. Retain the record for collection and the later response exercise. Assess question clarity, evidence gap, routing, and priority here; assess finished response writing in 2.7.4.

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

1. An RFI requests an answer. PIR designation and incident creation are separate organizational decisions.
2. Record the bounded question, available request evidence, missing transfer/host evidence, relevant source or owner, and priority rationale. Do not record successful delivery as established.
3. Clarify the actor or activity, environment, time window, decision/use, needed-by time, and handling constraints.
