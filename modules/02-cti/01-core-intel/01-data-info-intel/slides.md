### Slide 3 – Three terms
**Title:** Data, information, intelligence

**Data** — what was recorded.  
**Information** — the story (who / what / when / where).  
**Intelligence** — so what? What should someone do?

**Speaker Notes:**  
Outline a. Information describes. Intelligence judges.

---

### Slide 4 – What good looks like
**Title:** Same case, three layers

**Data** — `203.0.113.88`. Or the hash of Temp `invoice.vbs`.  
**Information** — that IP is the A for the update domain; file on **WS-JLEE**.  
**Intelligence** — we assess it is the payload host for **A12**; treat it as such.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Outline b and the task. Do not dump the Run key. Do not write a 2.11 paper.
