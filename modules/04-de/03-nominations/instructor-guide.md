## Module Overview for Instructors

**Purpose of this module:**  
Who can nominate, what the nomination must contain, and how DE reviews: accept, send back, or reject — plus who still owes what.

**Context (plain language):**

- What this hour is for: Other desks send DE work. That hand-off is a nomination. Need + pointer. Three reviews. Who finishes what.
- How it hooks to the hour before: 4.2 was sound, the shop list, and the close-the-loop note.
- How it hooks to the hour after: 4.4 is a tune request on a *live* rule. Same desk, different inbox.
- Why we are doing it this way: You wanted a nomination to name the need and point at an investigation or intel report. A drafted rule only if they have one — not required. Still not “ready to deploy.”
- What we are *not* doing this hour: Writing a rule (1.3). Testing (4.2). Tunes (4.4). Packages (4.5). No lab. No DYA tickets or forms.
- Extra step: none.

Say **need**, **pointer**, **accept for work**, **send back**, and **reject** the way the student guide does. **Report** means intel report. **Sent back** in 4.2 is the close-the-loop *note*. Here **send back** is the review.

**Key Teaching Points:**
- Only SOC, hunt, and CTI nominate.
- Clear enough = need + pointer. Drafted rule is optional.
- Always say who still owes what.

## Detailed Teaching Notes

### 1. Key Concepts

Write need + pointer. Then the three reviews. Walk the three “given” lines.

If they require a drafted rule to accept: “Not required. DE finishes.”  
If they invent a ticket or form: “Pointer *kinds* — investigation number, or report title and URL. Not a DYA form.”  
If they start writing a rule: “That is 1.3.”

---

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

1. **Who can nominate?**  
   **Answer:** A SOC analyst, a hunter, or a CTI analyst.  
   **Explanation:** Outline a.

2. **Need + pointer, no drafted rule — accept or send back?**  
   **Answer:** Accept. A drafted rule is not required. DE finishes the rule.  
   **Explanation:** Outline e.

3. **Need, no pointer?**  
   **Answer:** Send back. The nominator still owes the investigation number, or the report title and URL.  
   **Explanation:** Outline e and 4.3.1 tasks 1–2.
