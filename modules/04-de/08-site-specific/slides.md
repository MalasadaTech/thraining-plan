### Slide 2 – What this hour is
**Title:** What this hour is

Every shop has its own DE **policy**.

A **list** (requirements).  
A **path** (review, deploy, retire).

**Obtain** it. **Follow** only what you were shown.  
Do **not** invent it.

**Speaker Notes:**  
You wanted this hour to be about local policy existing — not a fake DYA handbook.

### Slide 4 – The path
**Title:** Review, deploy, retire

How a change is reviewed and deployed.  
How a retire is recorded.

Obtain the path. Do **not** invent a change board or a ticket name.

**Speaker Notes:**

### Slide 5 – Follow only what you were shown
**Title:** Have it / do not have it

Shown a list → align **only** to that list.  
No list or path → record that you do **not** have it yet.  
Invented “change board X” or a ticket name → **reject**.

**Speaker Notes:**  
Walk the three student-guide givens if you need them. The fail is inventing policy.
