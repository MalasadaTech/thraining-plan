## Module Overview for Instructors

**Purpose of this module:**  
Local policy exists. Obtain the list and the path. Follow only what you were shown. Reject inventing policy.

**Context (plain language):**

- What this hour is for: Every shop has its own DE policy — a requirements list, and a review / deploy / retire path. You obtain it. You do not invent DYA policy.
- How it hooks to the hour before: 4.7 was sensors. 4.2 already said the field *list* is local. This hour is obtain-and-follow for both the list and the path.
- How it hooks to the hour after: Section 4 is complete.
- Why we are doing it this way: You wanted 4.8.1 and 4.8.2 in one pass, lumped. Focus is that local policy exists. Do not invent it.
- What we are *not* doing this hour: Publishing a DYA field list. Naming a change board or ticket. Writing a rule. No lab.
- Extra step: none. Lumping 4.8.1 and 4.8.2 is what you asked for.

Say **list**, **path**, **obtain**, and **policy** the way the student guide does. **4.2** named kinds (meta fields, naming, IDs, tags, logging). Do not turn those kinds into a fake DYA list on the board.

**Key Teaching Points:**
- Local policy exists. It varies by shop.
- Have it / do not have it. Align only to a list you were shown.
- Inventing a change board or ticket name is a fail.

## Knowledge Check – Answer Key

1. **This course publishes the DYA list and path. True or false?**  
   **Answer:** False. Local policy varies by shop. Obtain it.  
   **Explanation:** 4.8.1 b and 4.8.2.

2. **No local list — invent the fields?**  
   **Answer:** No. Record that you do not have the list. Do not invent fields.  
   **Explanation:** 4.8.1.1 tasks 1–2.

3. **Invent a change board or ticket name as policy. Follow or reject?**  
   **Answer:** Reject.  
   **Explanation:** 4.8.2.2.
