**Target Audience:** Threat Hunter (primary), SOC Analyst and CTI Analyst (secondary)  
**Proficiency Focus:**

**Estimated Time:** 60–75 minutes  

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. State that **what a hunt write-up must include** and **where it lives** **varies by site**.
2. Treat finding that standard as **early orientation** — you do not invent a template as policy.
3. **Document** only in the store and on the form you were shown. If you were shown none, you **do not** declare a personal note “the record.”

**Mapped Proficiency Items:**
- K: 2.7.2 – Hunt documentation standards
- T: 2.7.2.1 – Document a hunt according to local standards

## 1. Key Concepts

**2.7.1** was how a hunt is *opened and controlled*. This hour is how it is **written down**. **2.2.2** already taught a classroom hunt card. That card is training, not your site’s form.

This course **does not** publish Harbor’s hunt template or the share it lives in. Every shop lists its own required elements and store.

**What exists locally (outline a–b) — not the names:**

| Path | You will be shown (locally) | You do **not** do this hour |
|------|-----------------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Required elements** | What the site form always wants (hypothesis, scope, findings, …) | Invent “Harbor Hunt Form v3” as policy |
| **Where** | Wiki / ticket / share your lead points at | Personal Drive / Slack as the official record |

**Orientation line:**  
`elements / store | who I asked | I have / do not have the local standard | next step`

**Document line:**  
`what I need to record | standard I was shown | I use it / I still need the standard / I invented a template (fail)`

If no one has shown you the standard, you **do not** promote a scratch note to “the hunt record.”

| This lesson | Other |
|-------------|-------|
| Find and use the *site* form and store | Hunt-card *ideas* — **2.2.2** |
| Not initiate / control | **2.7.1** |
| Not outputs / hand-off | **2.7.3** |
| Not SOC investigation notes | **1.8.4** |

| Expected (usually) | Lead (usually) |
|--------------------|----------------|
| “Asked lead; no template in this packet; scratch notes are not the record” | “Harbor wiki page Hunt-Template.md” as policy |
| Wait to file until the store is named | Drop the Run-key hunt in Slack and call it documented |

## 2. Detailed Walkthrough / Examples

**Work on the desk:** notes from the **2.6.3** Run-key hunt.

### Example 1: Honest Orientation (Expected)

**Ask:** your hunt lead.  
**Shown:** nothing in *this* packet.  
**Orientation:** `elements + store | lead | do not have the local standard | get the form before I file the Run-key hunt`  
**Document:** `Run-key notes | none shown | I still need the standard`

### Example 2: Invented Template (Lead)

**Draft:** “Harbor requires hypothesis, ATT&CK, screenshots, and a Confluence page titled Hunt-YYYY-MM.”

**Fail.** You wrote a standard this course does not own.  
**Lead:** Inventing the form is not “documenting to local standards.”

### Example 3: Slack Is the Record (Lead)

**Draft:** Paste the hunt into Slack. Call it done. Never asked where official hunts live.

**Fail.** Location is local (**outline b**). A chat thread is not the site store unless the lead *showed* you that.  
**Lead:** Ask where. Then file there.

## 3. Hands-On Exercise

**Objective:** Orient. Do not invent a hunt template. File only against a standard you were shown.

**This packet contains no local hunt-documentation standard. That is intentional.**

**Instructions:**

1. One sentence each for Examples 1–3.
2. Write an **orientation line** as a new arrival (who you would ask; that you do not have the standard in this packet).
3. **Document** lines for:

   - A. You have Run-key notes. No form or store shown.  
   - B. Your lead *in class* shows a real site form (if they do). File *that* way only. If they do not, write “still need the standard.”  
   - C. “I will use the 2.2.2 classroom card as this section’s official template.”

4. Do not invent a wiki path. Do not open **2.7.3** hand-off. Do not rewrite **2.6.3**.
5. Invented Harbor template text is a fail.

**Expected Outcome:**
- Three example summaries
- One orientation line (do not have the standard)
- Three document lines (A = need the standard; C = fail)
- No invented template
