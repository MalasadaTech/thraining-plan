## Delivery changes

- Initial platform orientation precedes enrichment. Full platform interpretation and performance checks are completed with the relevant enrichment method.
- RFI intake is 2.1.5; response writing and closure are 2.7.4.
- IOC handling is 2.5.1; correlation and campaign tracking are 2.5.7.
- Generic infrastructure reasoning is 2.5.5; DTF classification and next-lookup application are 2.5.6.
- TTP applicability and organizational significance are 2.6.1–2.6.2.
- STIX remains a distinct two-lesson unit in 2.7.1–2.7.2.
- Local requirements and permissions are referenced when first needed, then consolidated in 2.8.

All existing source files are retained. Prior review drafts and extraction records remain historical snapshots. Generated exports are intentionally unchanged pending the later export rebuild.
